Blog: Coproducts via Group Actions

Written by: Gabriel Field.

Goal (and prerequisite knowledge)

I want to motivate coproducts in $\mathbf{Group}$ (also known as free products) using the idea of group actions. As an essential prerequisite knowledge checklist:

  • The categories $\mathbf{Group}$ and $\mathbf{Set}$;
  • Group actions (on a set).

And some stuff that I'll assume you know later in the article, but which isn't essential for understanding:

  • The category $\mathbf{Top}_{*}$ of based topological spaces and continuous based maps;
  • The product topology.
  • The wedge sum operation.
