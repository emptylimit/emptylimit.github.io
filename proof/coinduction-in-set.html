Coinduction in the Category of Sets

Written by: Gabriel Field.

Theorem statement

Theorem. (Coinduction in $\mathbf{Set}$)

Let $J \xrightarrow{\texttt{des}} T\ J$ be the terminal coalgebra for an endofunctor $T : \mathbf{Set} \to \mathbf{Set}$. Let $\sim$ be an equivalence relation on $J$ and denote by $[-] : J \to J / {\sim}$ the projection onto its quotient. Suppose that for all $x, y \in J$ with $x \sim y$, we have $(T [-] \circ \texttt{des}) (x) = (T [-] \circ \texttt{des}) (y)$. Then, $\sim$ is the equality relation on $J$; i.e. for all $x, y \in J$, $x \sim y \implies x = y$.
