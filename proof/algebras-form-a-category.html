Algebras Form a Category

Written by: Gabriel Field.

Theorem statement

Theorem.

The algebras for an endofunctor $T : \mathsf{C} \to \mathsf{C}$ form a category, equipped with identity arrows $1_{T\ a \xrightarrow{\alpha} a} := 1_a$ and with composition of morphisms given by composition in $\mathsf{C}$.

Proof

It is easy to see that identity arrows in $\mathsf{C}$ are algebra morphisms. We next show that composites of algebra morphisms are again algebra morphisms.

Take algebras and algebra morphisms as displayed. Because the inner two squares commute, the whole diagram commutes. Thus, $g \circ f$ is an algebra morphism.

Because composition is associative and unital in $\mathsf{C}$, composition of algebra morphisms is associative and unital also. We thus have a category. $\square$
